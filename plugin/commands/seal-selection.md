---
name: seal-selection
description: Remind the user how to seal a highlighted secret in the composer.
---

Tell the user, briefly:

Highlight the secret in the composer and press **⌘⌥L** (command palette: **Keybox: Seal Selection**). The selection is replaced with a `«kb:name»` handle. Then send the message. Do not ask them to paste the raw value into chat.
